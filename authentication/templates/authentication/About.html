Society Management System (SMS)

A unified platform streamlining gatekeeper operations, resident visitor pre-approvals, and perimeter security through real-time digital registries.

Digital Pass Clearance

Replaces manual paper registers with instant search, timestamped check-ins, and accurate check-out logs.

Resident Pre-Approval

Residents can pre-authorize visitors and upload photos in advance, allowing rapid identity verification by gate guards.

Daily Shift Records

Security personnel can filter daily guest entries, monitor who is currently inside the premises, and record departures.

Community Visitor Guidelines

Delivery & Logistics Deliveries past 10:00 PM require resident confirmation before gate entry is permitted.
Cabs & Ride-Hailing Drop-offs and pick-ups are allowed up to building foyer. Drivers must not park in resident bays.
Contractors & Maintenance Work crews must sign in with vehicle plates and carry valid identity cards between 09:00 AM and 06:00 PM.
Overnight Visitors Residents are advised to pre-register overnight visitors to avoid midnight security verification delays.
